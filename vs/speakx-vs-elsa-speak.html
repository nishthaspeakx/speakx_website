SpeakX vs ELSA Speak: pronunciation drills vs conversation practice

ELSA Speak alternative for Indian learners: ELSA's pronunciation drills vs SpeakX's voice conversations with Sia, 7 Indian languages and ₹299/month.

Start Speaking with SpeakXJump to the comparison

Facts last verified 2026-10-12. Prices and ratings can change.

Quick answer

SpeakX is an AI English speaking app for Indian learners, made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram. You practice on voice calls with Sia, an AI speaking partner, and explanations are available in 7 Indian languages.

ELSA Speak is a US-based pronunciation coach from ELSA Corp. Its speech recognition scores how you say each syllable, and it builds a lesson path around the sounds you get wrong.

Pick SpeakX if you hesitate to speak, think in Hindi or a regional language, and want to hold real conversations about interviews, office life or family situations.

Pick ELSA Speak if you already speak fairly freely but want sound-by-sound pronunciation correction, a choice of accent, or IELTS and TOEFL preparation.

The two apps

What SpeakX and ELSA Speak are each built for

SpeakX

SpeakX treats speaking as the whole point, not a reward at the end of a lesson. You call Sia and talk about a situation you actually face: a job interview, a call with your child's teacher, a chat with a new colleague. When you get stuck, Sia explains in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam and slowly moves the conversation into English. About 15 minutes a day is the suggested routine, and a progress report tracks fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation together.

ELSA Speak

ELSA Speak (English Language Speech Assistant) starts from a different question: not what you say, but how it sounds. It begins with a pronunciation test, scores every syllable you produce, and builds a personalized path through its 8,000+ lessons. It has since added AI role-play coaches, a Speech Analyzer, exam preparation and a bilingual mode. Hindi is one of its display and translation languages; it offers no other Indian language.

How they work

Two different approaches to getting you speaking

How SpeakX works

SpeakX is built around a daily voice call with Sia. You speak, Sia replies, and the conversation moves the way a real one would. Roleplays cover a job interview, an office conversation about a promotion, a parent-teacher meeting, a phone call. Premium unlocks more than 100 of these scenarios and removes the cap on AI talk time.

The part that matters most for hesitant speakers is the language bridge. If Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam is the language you think in, Sia explains in that language first and shifts the conversation into English step by step. You never have to understand an English explanation of English before you can start. Learning paths are mapped to CEFR levels from Pre-A1 up to advanced.

Feedback arrives as a progress report rather than a single number. It covers fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, and tells you what to work on next. A Confidence Meter tracks how freely you are speaking, and city leaderboards add friendly competition. The recommended habit is about 15 minutes a day.

One note on names: SpeakX is not the same as Speak (speak.com), a separate US company with its own app. SpeakX has 1 crore+ Google Play downloads with a 4.5 rating from 92.9K reviews, and received the ET Education Excellence Award 2026 for Excellence in Generative AI for Education.

How ELSA Speak works

ELSA Speak was founded in 2015, according to press coverage, and is run by ELSA Corp from California with an operations office in Vietnam. Its core technology is speech recognition tuned for non-native speakers. You read aloud and the app scores each syllable, showing which sounds were off. You can choose an American, British or Australian accent as your target.

You begin with a pronunciation test. From the results, ELSA builds an individual path through more than 8,000 lessons, organized in levels the app calls planets, shaped by your goals, progress, preferred accent and industry. A bilingual mode and native-language explanations exist for beginners, and Hindi is listed among the display and translation languages. Tamil, Telugu and other Indian languages are not.

Conversation practice has been layered on top of the drills: AI role-play coaches for Workplace, Interviews, Dating, Meetings and Presentations, open-ended dialogs, a grammar coach and career-specific vocabulary sets. A Speech Analyzer produces detailed reports with predicted exam scores, and Premium includes preparation for IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC and EIKEN. Everything is AI only; the FAQ states there are no live classes or human teachers.

ELSA is a global product; its site is offered in Vietnamese, Japanese, Indonesian, Portuguese, Spanish, Chinese and Thai. On Google Play it shows 1 crore+ downloads and a 4.2 rating from about 1.01 million reviews, a very large review base at a lower average than SpeakX. On the India App Store it is rated 4.6 from 15,649 ratings, and the listing notes that the app contains ads.

Key differences

The 5 differences that matter most

  1. 1

    Where you start: a conversation or a pronunciation test

    SpeakX puts you on a voice call with Sia on day one and lets the conversation surface what to fix. ELSA Speak starts with a pronunciation test and builds your path from the sounds you got wrong. They suit different problems. If your problem is freezing, start by talking. If your problem is being misheard, start by measuring.

  2. 2

    Seven Indian languages versus Hindi only

    SpeakX explains in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam and moves you into English gradually. ELSA Speak supports Hindi as a display and translation language, which helps Hindi speakers, but a Tamil or Telugu speaker has to work in English or Hindi. For a large part of India this single difference decides the choice.

  3. 3

    How pronunciation feedback is delivered

    ELSA's feedback is granular: a score for each syllable, against a target accent you choose. SpeakX gives pronunciation feedback inside a broader progress report, next to fluency, grammar and vocabulary. ELSA tells you more about a single sound; SpeakX tells you more about whether the whole conversation worked.

  4. 4

    Scenarios: Indian daily life versus global career

    SpeakX roleplays include parent-teacher meetings, phone calls and office promotions, situations written for Indian adults. ELSA's role-play coaches cover Workplace, Interviews, Meetings, Presentations and Dating, with career-specific vocabulary and exam tracks. ELSA goes deeper on professional and exam English; SpeakX goes wider on everyday Indian situations.

  5. 5

    One price versus a ladder of plans

    SpeakX Premium is a single plan: ₹2 for a 3-day trial, then ₹299 a month on the SpeakX website as of 12 October 2026. ELSA offers a 7-day trial and then a range of Pro and Premium plans; the India App Store shows ₹499 a month up to ₹12,900 a year. ELSA's trial is longer; its monthly price is higher and the plan ladder takes more reading.

Honest fit

Where each app is the better choice

  • ✓Learners who understand English but freeze when they have to speak it, and need a partner that keeps the conversation going instead of stopping to grade each word.
  • ✓Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam speakers who want explanations in their own language before switching to English.
  • ✓Practicing specific Indian situations out loud: interviews, office conversations, parent-teacher meetings, phone calls.
  • ✓Absolute beginners starting at CEFR Pre-A1 who need a gentle, step-by-step path rather than a pronunciation test on day one.
  • ✓Anyone who wants one simple plan at ₹299 a month after a ₹2 three-day trial, and a 15-minute daily habit backed by a Confidence Meter and city leaderboards.

Where ELSA Speak may be a better fit

  • ✓Syllable-level pronunciation analysis, with a chosen target accent (American, British or Australian), for learners whose main problem is being misheard.
  • ✓Exam candidates: IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC and EIKEN preparation tracks and a Speech Analyzer with predicted scores are part of ELSA Premium.
  • ✓Learners who want a formal placement test and a learning path generated from it, since ELSA's pronunciation test does exactly that.
  • ✓People who want to practice on a laptop as well as a phone: ELSA's Speech Analyzer is available as a web tool.
  • ✓Organizations: ELSA offers school and enterprise programs, and a longer 7-day trial for individuals who want more time to evaluate.

Experience

User experience and how each app gives feedback

SpeakX's feedback is conversational. During a call, Sia keeps the exchange going and nudges you when you stumble, rather than stopping to grade each word. Afterwards, the progress report scores fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation and tells you what to work on next. On Premium, a 24x7 AI teacher gives instant feedback. The Confidence Meter tracks whether you are actually speaking more freely, which a pronunciation score cannot measure.

ELSA's feedback is diagnostic. Say a sentence and you get a score for each syllable and a model to copy in your chosen accent. The Speech Analyzer extends this to longer speech and produces detailed reports, including predicted exam scores. Progress is shown in quantitative dashboards; the Play listing calls it superior quantitative progress tracking. ELSA is strongest when the question is measurable.

The trade-off matters for Indian learners with mother-tongue influence (MTI). ELSA scores the sounds MTI affects most, such as a retroflex t and d, v and w, or stress on the wrong syllable, and drills them until the score rises. SpeakX notices pronunciation problems too, but treats them as one of four things to fix, and will not let you spend a week on a single sound while your sentences stay stuck in your head.

On outcomes, SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently; that is the company's own claim. ELSA's site calls itself the number one AI English speaking app and claims 92 million+ downloads, also company claims. The figures we can verify are the store ratings: 4.5 from 92.9K Google Play reviews for SpeakX and 4.2 from about 1.01 million for ELSA, as displayed on 12 October 2026.

FAQ

SpeakX vs ELSA Speak: common questions

Is ELSA Speak or SpeakX better for Indian learners?+

SpeakX fits most Indian learners who are still building the habit of speaking, because it explains in 7 Indian languages and practices Indian situations on voice calls. ELSA Speak fits better if you already speak reasonably well and want precise pronunciation correction or exam preparation. Some learners start with SpeakX and add ELSA later for polish.

Should I practice pronunciation or conversation first?+

If you freeze or translate in your head before speaking, practice conversation first; no pronunciation score helps a sentence you never say. If you already speak freely but people ask you to repeat yourself, pronunciation drills will give you faster results. Most Indian learners sit in the first group at the start and move to the second within a few months.

What are ELSA Speak alternatives that explain in Hindi?+

SpeakX explains in Hindi and six other Indian languages (Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam) and shifts the conversation into English gradually. ELSA itself supports Hindi as a display and translation language, so Hindi speakers are not locked out of it. If you need Tamil, Telugu or another regional language, ELSA does not list them, and SpeakX does.

Does SpeakX give pronunciation feedback?+

Yes. Pronunciation is one of the four areas in the SpeakX progress report, alongside fluency, vocabulary and grammar, and Sia corrects you during voice calls. It does not score each syllable the way ELSA does, so if you want sound-level detail and an accent target, ELSA goes deeper. SpeakX's aim is intelligibility inside a real conversation.

How does ELSA Speak pricing compare with SpeakX in India?+

SpeakX Premium is ₹2 for a 3-day trial and then ₹299 a month on the SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026. ELSA offers a 7-day trial, after which the India App Store shows plans from ₹499 a month up to ₹12,900 a year, with several Pro and Premium tiers in between. ELSA's trial is longer; SpeakX's monthly price is lower and simpler.

Is ELSA Speak good for conversation practice?+

It has become reasonable at it. ELSA offers AI role-play coaches for Workplace, Interviews, Dating, Meetings and Presentations, plus open-ended dialogs with instant feedback. Its own materials still lead with pronunciation scoring, with conversation alongside the drills rather than at the center. SpeakX is built the other way round.

Can I use ELSA Speak in Tamil or Telugu?+

No. ELSA's help-center language lists include Hindi but not Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali, so you would work in English or Hindi. SpeakX supports all seven of those Indian languages for explanations.
