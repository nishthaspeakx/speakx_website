SpeakX vs MySivi: AI spoken English apps compared

MySivi alternative? Compare SpeakX and MySivi on AI conversation practice, feedback, Indian-language explanations and pricing for Indian learners.

Quick answer

SpeakX is an AI English speaking app for Indian learners, made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram. You practice on voice calls with Sia, its AI speaking partner, in real-life roleplays for about 15 minutes a day, with explanations in seven Indian languages.

MySivi is a Bengaluru-based, Y Combinator-backed AI English speaking app built around an AI coach with topic-based chats and tasks, a guided lesson path, word translations in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali and Urdu, streaks and badges, and IELTS, TOEFL and placement goals.

Pick SpeakX if you want to speak out loud on a call, be guided in your own language from Pre-A1 upwards, and pay ₹299 a month after a ₹2 three-day trial (SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026).

Pick MySivi if exam-aware practice, streaks, Urdu translation or offline modules matter more, and its India App Store price of ₹999 a month (as of 12 October 2026) fits your budget.

The two apps

What SpeakX and MySivi are each built for

SpeakX

SpeakX is built around one habit: a daily voice call with Sia, an AI speaking partner that plays the interviewer, your manager, a class teacher or the person on a phone call. Explanations start in your Indian language and shift to English as you improve. It is free to start, has 1 crore+ Google Play downloads and shows a 4.5 rating from 92.9K reviews (as of 12 October 2026).

MySivi

MySivi (formerly Sivi and, before that, Speakify AI) is an AI English coach app from a Y Combinator W22 startup in Bengaluru. Its Play listing describes topic-based chats and real-time tasks with instant corrections, a structured learning path, word translations in six-plus Indian languages, offline modules, and badges and streaks. It also has 1 crore+ Play downloads and displays a 4.7 rating from 88.6K reviews (as of 12 October 2026).

How they work

Two different approaches to getting you speaking

How SpeakX works

SpeakX treats spoken English as a speaking problem, not a reading problem. Each day you take a voice call with Sia, the AI speaking partner, who plays the interviewer, your manager, your child's class teacher or the person on a phone call. You answer out loud, in real time, as you would with a human. A session takes about 15 minutes.

The app is designed for learners who hesitate. Explanations begin in your own language, whether Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam, and the conversation moves to English step by step. Paths are mapped to CEFR levels from Pre-A1 up to advanced, so a first-time speaker and a working professional are not given the same conversation.

After you speak, SpeakX keeps a progress report across fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, with a Confidence Meter and city leaderboards for motivation. Premium adds a 24x7 AI teacher with instant feedback, 100+ real-life scenarios and unlimited AI talk time. The app is free to start; Premium is a 3-day trial for ₹2, then ₹299 a month on the SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026.

SpeakX was recognized at the ET Education Excellence Awards 2026 for Excellence in Generative AI for Education. Its outcome claim is its own and is quoted as such later on this page.

How MySivi works

MySivi calls itself "Your Personalized English Speaking App to Improve English Fluency" on Google Play. The core is an AI English coach that, in the listing's words, listens to you, corrects mistakes and gives instant feedback. Practice runs through topic-based chats and real-time tasks. Voice-call style practice is not spelled out on the listing, so we describe MySivi as chat-and-task led based on the official copy available to us.

Around that coach sit a structured learning path with personalized lessons, grammar improvements and fluency tips, pronunciation support, and offline modules. For Indian learners there are word translations in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Urdu and more. The listing mixes in Hindi, telling learners to "har conversation mein confidently English bole".

MySivi leans into exams and placements more explicitly than SpeakX: the Play copy names IELTS, TOEFL, job interviews and placements as goals, and it uses badges, streaks and points openly. Y Combinator's company page says MySivi believes practicing with both an AI tutor and humans is the fastest route, and an April 2024 Inc42 profile described a peer-learning feature and an AI tutor called ARYA. We could not verify professional human tutors.

Some things remain unverified because MySivi's website blocked automated access during our check (12 October 2026). A placement test, a progress report, a scenario catalog and website pricing could not be confirmed from official sources. Where this page says "not verified", it means the Play or App Store listings do not describe the feature, not that MySivi lacks it.

Key differences

The 5 differences that matter most

  1. 1

    Voice calls versus chats and tasks

    SpeakX's practice unit is a voice call with Sia, where you speak continuously in a roleplay. MySivi's listing describes topic-based chats and real-time tasks with an AI coach that corrects you. If you freeze when you have to talk, the call format makes you speak; if you prefer short task-based turns, MySivi may feel lighter.

  2. 2

    Explanations in your language versus word translations

    SpeakX teaches in seven Indian languages and gradually shifts the whole conversation to English. MySivi translates words into Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Urdu and more while the practice runs in English. MySivi covers Urdu; SpeakX covers Kannada and Malayalam.

  3. 3

    CEFR paths versus a guided path with exam focus

    SpeakX maps its paths to CEFR levels from Pre-A1 to advanced, so a hesitant beginner knows where to start. MySivi describes a structured, personalized learning path and names IELTS, TOEFL and placements as goals. A placement test on MySivi is not verified from official sources.

  4. 4

    Progress report versus instant corrections

    SpeakX gives a progress report across fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation plus a Confidence Meter. MySivi's listing promises instant corrections and fluency tips during practice; a progress report is not verified on its official listings.

  5. 5

    ₹299 versus ₹999 a month

    SpeakX Premium is ₹299 a month after a ₹2 three-day trial on the SpeakX website (India, 12 October 2026). MySivi's India App Store in-app purchases on the same date are ₹999 for one month, ₹1,999 for three months and ₹4,999 for a year. MySivi's website prices could not be fetched, so other plans may exist.

Honest fit

Where each app is the better choice

Where SpeakX may be a better fit

  • ✓Learners who freeze when speaking: Sia keeps the conversation going on a voice call rather than waiting for a typed or short task answer.
  • ✓Hindi-medium and regional-language learners who want explanations, not just translations, in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam.
  • ✓Absolute beginners, because paths start at CEFR Pre-A1 and the app says you do not need perfect English to start.
  • ✓Indian life situations: parent-teacher meetings, office conversations, phone calls and interviews are built-in roleplays, with 100+ scenarios on Premium.
  • ✓Budget-conscious learners: ₹299 a month after a ₹2 trial is roughly a third of MySivi's ₹999 App Store monthly price (both India, 12 October 2026).
  • ✓Learners who want to see improvement in numbers: a progress report on fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, plus a Confidence Meter.

Where MySivi may be a better fit

  • ✓IELTS, TOEFL and placement candidates: MySivi names these goals directly on its listing; SpeakX does not describe exam-specific prep.
  • ✓Urdu speakers: MySivi lists Urdu among its translation languages; SpeakX's seven languages do not include Urdu.
  • ✓Learners who like streaks and badges: MySivi's badges, streaks and points are a core part of the experience.
  • ✓Practice without a steady connection: MySivi's listing mentions offline modules, which SpeakX does not describe.
  • ✓Learners who weigh store ratings heavily: MySivi displays 4.7 from 88.6K Google Play reviews versus SpeakX's 4.5 from 92.9K (12 October 2026).

Experience

User experience and how each app gives feedback

On SpeakX, feedback is tied to the call. Sia listens to the whole conversation, and after the session you see where you hesitated and how your grammar and pronunciation held up. Over days this builds a progress report across fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, and the Confidence Meter shows how comfortable you are becoming. Premium adds a 24x7 AI teacher with instant feedback.

The outcome claim is the company's own: SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently. Treat it as a company statement, not an independent finding.

MySivi's feedback, as described on Google Play, is immediate and correction-led. The AI coach corrects mistakes as you go, offers grammar improvements and fluency tips, and gives personalized feedback on lessons. Word translations into Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali or Urdu remove the friction of not knowing a word mid-practice. Streaks, badges and points reward consistency.

What we could not verify for MySivi is the long view: a progress report, a placement test and a scenario catalog are not described on its official listings. If a trend line of your speaking matters, SpeakX's progress report is the clearer offer today; if in-the-moment corrections and rewards matter more, MySivi's listing speaks directly to that.

FAQ

SpeakX vs MySivi: common questions

What is a good MySivi alternative for spoken English?+

SpeakX is one option, as are other India-focused AI apps such as Supernova and Stimuler. Rather than hunting for a single "best" app, check four things: whether practice is spoken or mostly typed chat, whether the app explains in your language or only translates words, whether it tracks progress over weeks, and the monthly price on the platform you will use. On those checks SpeakX offers voice calls with Sia, seven Indian languages, a progress report and ₹299 a month after a ₹2 trial.

SpeakX or MySivi: which gives voice feedback during conversation?+

SpeakX's practice is a voice call with Sia, with feedback on fluency, grammar, vocabulary and pronunciation after the conversation. MySivi's Play listing says its AI coach listens, corrects mistakes and gives instant feedback through topic-based chats and real-time tasks; whether that happens inside a continuous voice call is not explicit on its official listings.

Does MySivi or SpeakX explain in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali?+

Both support these languages, differently. SpeakX teaches in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam, starting in your language and shifting to English over time. MySivi offers word translations in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Urdu and more while you practice in English. For Kannada or Malayalam, SpeakX lists them; for Urdu, MySivi does.

Which app is better for Indian learners who want AI conversation practice?+

It depends on the conversation you want. SpeakX is built for speaking out loud: voice calls with Sia in roleplays like interviews, office talk and parent-teacher meetings, mapped to CEFR levels from Pre-A1. MySivi is built around an AI coach with topic-based chats and tasks, a guided path, and exam and placement goals. Try each free for a week and notice which one you actually speak more in.

How does SpeakX pricing compare with MySivi?+

SpeakX Premium costs ₹299 a month after a 3-day trial for ₹2 on the SpeakX website (India, 12 October 2026). MySivi's India App Store in-app purchases on the same date are ₹999 for one month, ₹1,999 for three months and ₹4,999 for a year; its website pricing could not be fetched, so other plans may exist. Both apps are free to download and start.

Is MySivi free to use?+

MySivi's Play listing says many features are available free, and a 2024 Inc42 profile reported 30 minutes of free practice a day at that time. SpeakX is also free to start, with Premium unlocking unlimited AI talk time and 100+ scenarios; its trial is not free, it costs ₹2 for three days.

Is MySivi the same as Sivi or Speakify? And is SpeakX the same as Speak?+

MySivi was earlier called Sivi and, before that, Speakify AI; it is the same product under the App Store developer Ninjasalary, Inc. Be careful with "Sivi" alone, since an unrelated design tool uses that name. Separately, SpeakX is not Speak (speak.com); they are different companies.

Does MySivi help with IELTS or TOEFL preparation?+

MySivi's Play listing says it boosts confidence for IELTS, TOEFL, job interviews and placements, so exam speaking confidence is a stated goal; band-score prediction or a formal exam module is not verified from official sources. SpeakX does not describe IELTS or TOEFL-specific prep.
