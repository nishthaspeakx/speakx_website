SpeakX vs Duolingo: which helps you speak English?

Duolingo alternative for English speaking in India: compare SpeakX's voice-call practice with Sia against Duolingo lessons, plus price and languages.

Start Speaking with SpeakXJump to the comparison

Facts last verified 2026-10-12. Prices and ratings can change.

Quick answer

First, a quick clarification: SpeakX is not Speak, the US app often compared with Duolingo; this page is about SpeakX, the Indian app.

SpeakX is an AI English speaking app for Indian learners, made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram, built around daily voice calls with Sia, an AI speaking partner, with explanations in seven Indian languages.

Duolingo is a free, gamified app that teaches 40 or more languages through short lessons, with English available as a course for Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu speakers.

Pick SpeakX if your goal is to speak English confidently for interviews, work or daily life; pick Duolingo if you want a free daily habit for vocabulary and grammar basics, or you are learning more than one language.

The two apps

What SpeakX and Duolingo are each built for

SpeakX

SpeakX does one job: it gets Indian adults talking in English. You call Sia, the AI speaking partner, for about 15 minutes a day. Sia explains in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam, then slowly moves the conversation into English. Practice happens inside roleplays such as job interviews, office conversations, parent-teacher meetings and phone calls. The app is free to start on Google Play and the App Store, and the Play listing shows 1 crore+ downloads and a 4.5 star rating from about 92.9K reviews.

Duolingo

Duolingo is probably the most recognized language app in the world. Its Google Play listing shows 500M+ downloads and a 4.6 rating from about 50.9 million reviews, and its Q2 2026 filing reports 58.7 million daily active users. Every language course is free with ads. Lessons are short, game-like exercises in reading, writing, listening and speaking, held together by streaks, XP, hearts and leagues. English is one of 40 or more courses, and the AI conversation features, Roleplay and Video Call with Lily, sit inside the paid Duolingo Max tier.

How they work

Two different approaches to getting you speaking

How SpeakX works

SpeakX treats speaking as the whole lesson, not one exercise at the end. The core activity is a voice call with Sia, an AI speaking partner that listens, replies and keeps the conversation going. You do not need good English to begin: the Play listing says you do not need perfect English to start, and the CEFR-mapped paths begin at Pre-A1.

The second idea is language support that fades out. Sia explains in your own language, whether Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam, and gradually shifts the conversation across to English as you become comfortable. The explanation and the speaking happen in the same conversation, which feels very different from a translation-based lesson.

Practice is organized around Indian situations: job interviews, office conversations and promotions, parent-teacher meetings, phone calls, travel and everyday social talk. You can start on the free tier; Premium adds a 24x7 AI teacher with instant feedback, 100 or more real-life scenarios and unlimited AI talk time.

Feedback arrives as a progress report that scores fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation and tells you what to work on next. A Confidence Meter and city leaderboards add light motivation. The recommended routine is about 15 minutes a day on your phone; there is no web version.

How Duolingo works

Duolingo organizes each language into a path of units. Each lesson is a run of short exercises: match the word, translate the sentence, type what you hear, repeat a phrase aloud. Speaking is one exercise type among several, and the speech recognition checks whether you said the phrase, not how you handle an open conversation. English courses exist from Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu, and the interface is available in Hindi, Bengali, Kannada, Marathi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and Urdu.

The habit layer is where Duolingo stands out. Streaks reward daily use, XP feeds weekly leagues, achievements mark milestones, and hearts cap how many mistakes you can make before you pause or practice to recover. Many people who have never finished a course on any app have a long Duolingo streak, and that consistency is real value.

All of this is free, supported by ads. Super Duolingo removes ads and gives unlimited hearts, and the Play listing mentions a 14-day free trial. Duolingo Max, the higher tier, adds AI features: Roleplay for practice conversations, Explain My Answer for grammar explanations, and Video Call with the character Lily. These launched in 2023 and cover selected courses, so check whether your English course includes them before you pay.

Duolingo runs on Android, iOS and the web, with progress synced across devices, and the same account offers math, music and chess. The product is designed for a global audience, so the English content is general rather than tuned to Indian interviews, offices or accents. Job interview and workplace English tracks are not verified from official sources.

Key differences

The 5 differences that matter most

  1. 1

    Conversation practice: voice calls with Sia vs Duolingo exercises

    On SpeakX, the conversation is the product. Every session is a voice call with Sia, and the free tier lets you start talking straight away. On Duolingo, speaking is one exercise type inside a lesson, and open conversation through Roleplay or Video Call is a Duolingo Max feature for selected courses. If you can read English but freeze when you speak, this is the difference that matters most.

  2. 2

    Explanations in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam

    Both apps meet Indian learners in their own language, but differently. Duolingo offers English courses from Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu, and an interface in eight Indian languages, a wider UI footprint. SpeakX uses seven Indian languages for spoken explanations inside the conversation itself, then shifts you into English. Marathi, Kannada and Malayalam speakers get explanations on SpeakX, while Duolingo's English courses are not listed from those languages.

  3. 3

    Interview, office and daily-life roleplays

    SpeakX roleplays are written for Indian life: a job interview, asking for a promotion, a parent-teacher meeting, a phone call, with 100 or more scenarios on Premium. Duolingo's Roleplay is a Max feature with general scenarios, and job interview or workplace English content is not verified from official sources. For someone preparing for placements or a new office, SpeakX is more direct.

  4. 4

    Daily time and habit: about 15 minutes a day

    SpeakX asks for about 15 minutes a day of actual speaking. Duolingo asks for a lesson or two a day and makes it very hard to skip, thanks to streaks and leagues. Duolingo is better at making you show up; SpeakX is better at making the time count as speaking practice. Decide which of those two problems is yours.

  5. 5

    Using Duolingo and SpeakX together

    These apps do different jobs, so many learners can use both. A practical routine: do your Duolingo English lesson for vocabulary and grammar drills and to keep your streak, then spend 15 minutes on a SpeakX call with Sia using that vocabulary out loud in a roleplay. Duolingo gives you the words; SpeakX gives you a place to say them.

Honest fit

Where each app is the better choice

Where SpeakX may be a better fit

  • ✓Indian adults who understand English but hesitate to speak it, and want real voice conversation from day one.
  • ✓Learners who want explanations in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam while they speak.
  • ✓Job seekers and freshers preparing for interviews, group discussions and first-day office conversations.
  • ✓Working professionals who need workplace English: meetings, phone calls, asking for a promotion.
  • ✓Parents and homemakers who want to handle parent-teacher meetings, shops and official phone calls in English.
  • ✓People who want a single, transparent INR price: ₹2 for a 3-day trial, then ₹299 a month, as of 12 October 2026.

Where Duolingo may be a better fit

  • ✓Anyone who wants a completely free app: every Duolingo language course is free with ads, with no payment needed to keep going.
  • ✓People who struggle to build a habit, because streaks, leagues and achievements are the most refined habit mechanics in the category.
  • ✓Learners who want more than one language, or math, music and chess, on one account.
  • ✓People who learn at a desk, since Duolingo works on the web as well as Android and iOS.
  • ✓Learners who value a very large community and a global, well-funded product: 500M+ Play downloads and 140.6 million monthly active users in Q2 2026.

Experience

User experience and how each app gives feedback

SpeakX gives feedback the way a patient tutor would. During a call, Sia keeps the conversation moving and, on Premium, acts as a 24x7 AI teacher that corrects you as you go. After practice you get a progress report that scores fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation and points to what to work on next. A Confidence Meter tracks how freely you are speaking over time, and city leaderboards show how your practice compares with learners nearby. SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently; treat that as a company claim rather than an independent result.

Duolingo's feedback is immediate and binary inside each exercise: right or wrong, with the correct answer shown. The emotional feedback is where it shines. The owl reminders, streak freezes, league promotions and achievement badges are designed to make you open the app tomorrow, and they work for a lot of people. Grammar is taught mostly implicitly; the Explain My Answer feature that spells out why you were wrong is part of Duolingo Max.

On speaking specifically, Duolingo's standard exercises check that you repeated a phrase; they do not score fluency or coach pronunciation in detail. Roleplay and Video Call in Max give AI feedback on a conversation, but for selected courses and at the paid tier. On SpeakX, every call is open conversation and every report covers pronunciation. A fair reading: Duolingo is better at telling you that you showed up and got the exercise right, and SpeakX is better at telling you how your spoken English is changing. If you use both, let Duolingo own the streak and SpeakX own the speaking.

FAQ

SpeakX vs Duolingo: common questions

Is SpeakX better than Duolingo for speaking English?+

For spoken English specifically, SpeakX is the more direct tool, because every session is a voice call with Sia and every progress report scores fluency and pronunciation. Duolingo's speaking exercises are short repeat-after-me checks, and its open AI conversation features sit in the paid Max tier for selected courses. Duolingo remains the better choice if you want a free app, a strong daily habit or more than one language.

Is Duolingo enough to learn to speak English?+

Duolingo is a good way to build vocabulary, basic grammar and a daily learning habit for free. On its own, the standard lessons give you limited open speaking practice, which is why many Duolingo users can read English but still hesitate to talk. If speaking is your goal, add real conversation practice, whether a speaking partner, a class or an app such as SpeakX.

Which Duolingo alternative is made for Indian learners?+

SpeakX is built specifically for Indian learners by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram. It explains in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam, uses Indian roleplays such as interviews and parent-teacher meetings, and prices in rupees. Duolingo also supports Indian learners with English courses from Hindi, Bengali, Tamil and Telugu, but its content is global rather than India-specific.

Does Duolingo have voice conversation practice?+

Yes, with limits. Standard Duolingo lessons include speaking exercises where you repeat phrases and the app checks them with speech recognition. Open voice conversation comes through Roleplay and Video Call with Lily, which are Duolingo Max features for selected courses. SpeakX, by comparison, makes voice calls with Sia the main activity, including on the free tier.

Is SpeakX the same as the Speak app that competes with Duolingo?+

No. Speak is a US company at speak.com and is the app most often compared with Duolingo in global reviews. SpeakX is a separate Indian company, Ivypods Technology, with its own app built around Sia and seven Indian languages. If you searched for SpeakX vs Duolingo and found Speak vs Duolingo articles, that is why.

Can I use Duolingo and SpeakX together?+

Yes, and the two fit well because they do different jobs. Use Duolingo for daily vocabulary and grammar drills and to keep your streak, then spend about 15 minutes on a SpeakX call using those words in a roleplay. Both apps are free to start, so you can try the combination before paying for either.

How much do SpeakX and Duolingo cost in India?+

SpeakX is free to start; Premium is ₹2 for a 3-day trial and then ₹299 per month on the SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026. Duolingo's India prices are not published on its public pages; its Play listing shows in-app purchases from ₹19 to ₹19,900 per item and a 14-day free trial of Super Duolingo. All Duolingo language courses are free with ads.
