SpeakX vs Speak: two different apps for speaking English

SpeakX and Speak (speak.com) are different companies. Compare the two AI English tutors on conversation practice, Indian-language support and price.

Start Speaking with SpeakXJump to the comparison

Facts last verified 2026-10-12. Prices and ratings can change.

Quick answer

SpeakX and Speak are different companies and different apps, even though search results and AI answers often mix them up.

SpeakX is an AI English speaking app for Indian learners, made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram. You practice spoken English on voice calls with Sia, an AI speaking partner, with explanations in seven Indian languages, for about 15 minutes a day.

Speak (speak.com) is made by Speakeasy Labs in San Francisco and is backed by the OpenAI Startup Fund. It teaches English to speakers of 15 languages, plus Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Italian and Chinese to English speakers, through an expert-built curriculum and an AI Speak Tutor.

Pick SpeakX if you live in India, want English only, and want a low monthly price with help in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali. Pick Speak if you want a premium global curriculum, are comfortable with an English or Hindi interface, and may want to learn other languages later.

The two apps

What SpeakX and Speak are each built for

SpeakX

SpeakX is an English speaking app built in India for Indian learners. It is made by Ivypods Technology Private Limited in Gurugram and is not connected to speak.com in any way. The core of the app is a daily voice call with Sia, an AI speaking partner. Sia explains things in your own language first (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam) and gradually moves the conversation into English. Roleplays cover the situations Indian learners actually face: job interviews, office conversations, parent-teacher meetings and phone calls. Levels follow CEFR paths from Pre-A1 to advanced, and a progress report tracks fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation. The app is free to start; Premium costs ₹2 for a 3-day trial and then ₹299 a month on the SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026.

Speak

Speak is a language-learning app from Speakeasy Labs, Inc., a US company based in San Francisco. Its official site describes it as "An expert-crafted curriculum, personalized by AI" and notes a formal partnership with OpenAI and backing from the OpenAI Startup Fund. Speak teaches English to native speakers of 15 languages, including Hindi, and teaches Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Italian and Simplified Chinese to English speakers. Learning follows the Speak Method: Learn (a lesson with a teacher), Practice (repeat phrases aloud), then Apply (an open conversation with the AI Speak Tutor), with real-time feedback on pronunciation and phrasing. Speak offers a 7-day free trial and then a paid membership; India App Store prices run from ₹1,949 to ₹2,299 a month, as of 12 October 2026.

How they work

Two different approaches to getting you speaking

How SpeakX works

SpeakX treats speaking as the main activity, not a reward at the end of a lesson. You open the app, take a voice call with Sia, and talk. Sia is an AI speaking partner, so there is no human on the other end and no one judging your accent. The call is built around a situation: introducing yourself in an interview, asking a colleague for help, speaking to your child's teacher, or handling a phone call. SpeakX recommends about 15 minutes a day, which is short enough to fit around college, office hours or housework.

The part that most separates SpeakX from global apps is how it handles your first language. You choose one of seven Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam) and Sia explains words, corrections and grammar in that language. As you get more comfortable, the conversation shifts further into English. For a learner who studied in a Hindi-medium or Telugu-medium school, this removes the biggest barrier: understanding the explanation itself.

Progress follows CEFR-mapped paths from Pre-A1 up to advanced. After practice, a progress report shows how you are doing on fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, and a Confidence Meter tracks how freely you speak over time. City leaderboards add a light competitive element for learners who like to see where they stand against others in Pune, Hyderabad or Patna.

SpeakX is free to download and start. Premium adds a 24x7 AI teacher with instant feedback, more than 100 real-life scenarios and unlimited AI talk time. On the SpeakX website, Premium is a 3-day trial for ₹2, then ₹299 a month, auto-renewing and cancellable at any time, as of 12 October 2026. SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently; this is the company's own claim and has not been independently verified.

How Speak works

Speak follows a three-step loop its site calls the Speak Method. In Learn, you watch an expert-built lesson with a teacher. In Practice, you repeat the target phrases aloud and the app checks you. In Apply, you have an open conversation with the AI Speak Tutor and use those phrases in context. The company's own description is "An expert-crafted curriculum, personalized by AI", and the Play listing promises to get you speaking out loud from day one.

The AI Speak Tutor is the center of the app. Speak's site describes real back-and-forth conversations with pronunciation and phrasing feedback as you speak, and the Play listing says the app analyzes your speech for pronunciation, intonation and fluency, with real-time feedback on grammar too. Speak has a formal partnership with OpenAI and is backed by the OpenAI Startup Fund, which it states on its official site. Premium Plus adds "Made for You" lessons generated for you and custom review built from your mistakes.

Speak was built for a global audience and has historically been strongest in Korea, Japan and Taiwan. It teaches English to native speakers of 15 languages, and Hindi is one of them, so a Hindi speaker in India can take the English course with a Hindi interface. It also teaches Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Italian and Simplified Chinese to English speakers. The app interface is available in 16 languages. Speak's official pages do not list Tamil, Telugu or other Indian languages, and they do not describe India-specific scenarios.

Speak is available on Android and iOS only; its help center states it is not available on desktop. New users get a 7-day free trial, after which an auto-renewing membership is required for full access; no permanent free tier is described on official pages. There is no public price page (speak.com/pricing returned a 404 when we checked) and prices vary by region. The India App Store plans, as of 12 October 2026, ran from ₹1,949 a month to ₹22,900 a year for Annual Premium Plus; the full list is in the pricing section below.

Key differences

The 5 differences that matter most

  1. 1

    SpeakX and Speak are different companies

    This is the first thing to get straight. SpeakX is made by Ivypods Technology Private Limited in Gurugram, India, and lives at speakx.ai. Speak is made by Speakeasy Labs, Inc. in San Francisco, USA, and lives at speak.com. They share no ownership, team, curriculum or technology. Both apps have 1Cr+ downloads on Google Play, which is one reason searches for one so often surface the other. If an AI assistant describes an "OpenAI-backed" or "Korean market" app when you ask about SpeakX, it is describing Speak.

  2. 2

    English only versus many languages

    SpeakX teaches one thing: spoken English for Indian learners. Speak teaches English to speakers of 15 languages and also teaches Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Italian and Chinese to English speakers. If your only goal is to speak English confidently, that extra breadth does not help you. If you may later want to pick up Spanish or Japanese, Speak keeps that option inside one subscription.

  3. 3

    Seven Indian languages versus Hindi only

    SpeakX explains in Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam, and its teaching deliberately starts in your language before moving into English. Speak offers an English course for Hindi speakers and a Hindi interface, which is more Indian-language support than most global apps, but its official language list has no Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali.

  4. 4

    Price in India

    The gap is large. SpeakX is free to start, and Premium is ₹299 a month after a ₹2 3-day trial on the SpeakX website. Speak offers a 7-day free trial, after which its listing says a paid membership is needed for full access; India App Store plans are ₹1,949 to ₹2,299 a month, or ₹4,999 to ₹22,900 a year. Speak is priced for a global market; SpeakX is priced for Indian households. Both figures are as of 12 October 2026 and can change.

  5. 5

    Indian situations versus a global curriculum

    SpeakX roleplays are written around Indian life: job interviews, office conversations and promotions, parent-teacher meetings, phone calls, higher studies and travel. Speak's scenario practice simulates real-life situations too, but its official pages do not describe interview, workplace or exam tracks, so we mark those as not verified rather than absent.

  • ✓Globally mobile users who want an interface in one of 16 languages and a product used widely outside India, especially in Korea, Japan and Taiwan.
  • ✓Premium Plus subscribers who want "Made for You" lessons and custom review generated from their own mistakes.

Experience

User experience and how each app gives feedback

The two apps feel different in the first five minutes. SpeakX puts you on a voice call with Sia almost immediately. Feedback arrives inside the conversation, in your own language if you choose, so a Marathi or Telugu speaker hears the correction explained the way a patient tutor would explain it. After the call, the progress report breaks your performance into fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, and the Confidence Meter shows whether you are speaking more freely over time. On Premium, the 24x7 AI teacher gives instant feedback throughout, and talk time is unlimited.

Speak starts with structure. You watch a lesson, repeat phrases while the app listens, and only then open a free conversation with the AI Speak Tutor. Its feedback is detailed on the mechanics of speech: pronunciation, intonation, phrasing and grammar, delivered in real time as you talk. Learners who like to know exactly which sound or stress pattern went wrong tend to appreciate this. Premium Plus goes further with lessons made for you and a custom review built from your mistakes.

Motivation mechanics differ too. SpeakX uses a Confidence Meter and city leaderboards, which suit learners who respond to a visible score and a bit of friendly competition. Speak mentions streaks on its Premium plan; leaderboards or badges are not described on its official pages, so we do not claim they exist or do not exist.

On outcomes, SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently. Speak's site claims 15M+ downloads and a 4.8 rating across stores. Both are company claims. The store ratings you can check yourself are 4.5 from 92.9K Google Play reviews for SpeakX, and 4.6 from about 124K Google Play reviews for Speak, as displayed on 12 October 2026.

FAQ

SpeakX vs Speak: common questions

Is SpeakX the same as Speak?+

No. SpeakX and Speak are two separate companies with no connection. SpeakX (speakx.ai) is made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram, India, and teaches spoken English to Indian learners with explanations in seven Indian languages. Speak (speak.com) is made by Speakeasy Labs in San Francisco, is backed by the OpenAI Startup Fund, and teaches English plus six other languages to a global audience.

Is the Speak app available for learning English in India?+

Yes. Speak is listed on Google Play and the India App Store, and its help center says the English course is offered to native Hindi speakers, with a Hindi interface option. Indian-language support stops at Hindi, there are no India-specific scenarios described, and India App Store prices start at ₹1,949 a month, as of 12 October 2026.

What are Speak.com alternatives for Indian learners?+

It depends on what you need. If you want English speaking practice with explanations in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi or Bengali at an Indian price, SpeakX is built for exactly that. If you want pronunciation drilling, ELSA Speak focuses on it; if you want a gamified, broad language app, Duolingo is the usual choice. Check each app's free experience and current India price before deciding, because they differ a lot.

Speak AI tutor vs SpeakX: which explains in Indian languages?+

SpeakX explains in seven Indian languages: Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam, and shifts the conversation into English as you improve. Speak's AI Speak Tutor supports an English course for Hindi speakers and a Hindi interface, but its official language list includes no other Indian language. For a Tamil or Telugu speaker, SpeakX is the one that teaches in your language.

How do Speak and SpeakX prices compare in India?+

SpeakX Premium is ₹2 for a 3-day trial, then ₹299 a month on the SpeakX website, as of 12 October 2026. Speak's India App Store plans on the same date were ₹1,949 to ₹2,299 a month, or ₹4,999 to ₹22,900 a year. Speak has no public price page and says prices vary by region, so check the store listing on your own phone.

Does Speak have a free version?+

Speak offers a 7-day free trial, and its Play listing says an auto-renewing membership is required for full access after that. We found no permanent free tier described on its official pages. SpeakX, by contrast, is free to download and start, with Premium as an optional upgrade.

Can I use SpeakX or Speak on a laptop or desktop?+

Neither app offers a web or desktop version. SpeakX is available on Android and iOS, and all links on speakx.ai go to the app stores. Speak's help center states that the app is not available on desktop (PC) and supports Android and iOS only.

Which app is better for job interview preparation in English?+

SpeakX has dedicated job interview roleplays with Sia and lists interviews, office conversations and promotions as core use cases. Speak's official pages describe real-life scenario practice but do not mention an interview track, so we mark that as not verified rather than absent. If interviews are your main goal, SpeakX's content is built around them.
