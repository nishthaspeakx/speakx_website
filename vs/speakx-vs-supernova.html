SpeakX vs Supernova: which spoken English app fits you?

SpeakX vs Supernova AI for spoken English: voice calls with Sia, 7 Indian languages, roleplays, ₹299/month after a ₹2 trial. See how the two apps differ.

Start Speaking with SpeakXJump to the comparison

Facts last verified 2026-10-12. Prices and ratings can change.

Quick answer

SpeakX is an AI English speaking app for Indian learners, made by Ivypods Technology in Gurugram. Supernova AI: Spoken English is a structured spoken-English course app made by L8 Cones Interactive in Bengaluru.

SpeakX gets you talking from the first session through voice calls with Sia, its AI speaking partner, inside real-life roleplays, with explanations in 7 Indian languages.

Supernova teaches lesson-first: you learn one sentence pattern, translate short lines from your mother tongue, chat with its AI tutor Nova, and revise with spaced repetition over about 20 minutes a day.

Pick SpeakX if you want conversation-first practice with a published price (₹299/month after a ₹2 3-day trial). Pick Supernova if you prefer a fixed daily curriculum and do not mind finding the price inside the app.

The two apps

What SpeakX and Supernova are each built for

SpeakX

SpeakX is built around one habit: speaking English out loud every day for about 15 minutes. You call Sia, an AI speaking partner, and work through situations Indian adults actually face, such as a job interview, a conversation with your manager, a parent-teacher meeting or a phone call. Sia explains in your own language (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam) and slowly moves the conversation into English.

Supernova

Supernova AI: Spoken English, from L8 Cones Interactive in Bengaluru, describes itself as a structured English speaking course for beginners. Each day brings a personalized lesson of around 20 minutes in a fixed sequence: learn a sentence pattern, translate short lines from your language, hold a controlled chat with the AI tutor Nova, speak freely, then revisit the material through spaced repetition. Explanations are available in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and more.

How they work

Two different approaches to getting you speaking

How SpeakX works

SpeakX treats speaking as the lesson, not the reward at the end of it. You pick a scenario and start a voice call with Sia, an AI speaking partner, so there is no human on the other side and no one judging a wrong tense. Sia asks, you answer, Sia responds to what you actually said. Because the practice is spoken rather than typed, you build the reflex of forming English sentences out loud.

The language scaffolding matters for hesitant beginners. SpeakX teaches in 7 Indian languages (Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam) and shifts the conversation into English as you get comfortable. Paths are mapped to CEFR levels from Pre-A1 to advanced, so someone who cannot yet introduce themselves and someone preparing for a promotion discussion both have a starting point. The Play listing puts it plainly: you do not need perfect English to begin.

Roleplays come from Indian adult life: job interviews, office conversations, higher studies, travel, parent-teacher meetings, phone calls and everyday chats with neighbours or relatives. Premium opens 100+ scenarios, a 24x7 AI teacher with instant feedback, and unlimited AI talk time. The free version lets you try the voice calls before paying. One clarification: SpeakX is not the same company as Speak (speak.com), a US-based app with a similar name.

After each session, a Progress Report shows movement on fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation and points to what to work on next. A Confidence Meter and city leaderboards add a light competitive layer. The routine is about 15 minutes a day, short enough to fit around college, shifts, housework or a commute.

How Supernova works

Supernova's method is published on its own website, which makes it easy to describe. The company says you learn a language by speaking it out loud, but its route to speaking is a curriculum rather than an open conversation. Every day you get a personalized lesson of roughly 20 minutes. It begins with a single sentence pattern, explained in your mother tongue.

You then translate 100 to 300 short lines from your language into English using that pattern, so the structure becomes automatic before you use it in conversation. Next comes a controlled chat with Nova (also called Miss Nova), the 24/7 AI tutor, which keeps the conversation inside the pattern you just learned. A free-speaking or extempore round follows, without guardrails. Finally, spaced repetition brings the material back on days 1, 2, 4, 7, 14 and 30.

Nova gives instant grammar corrections, pronunciation help and personalized feedback, according to the App Store listing. The Play listing describes strands for listening to everyday speech, daily-conversation vocabulary, grammar for accurate speaking, and pronunciation work on sound clarity, stress and rhythm. Role-plays covering introductions, interviews and everyday situations are listed, though the number of scenarios is not published.

Supernova offers explanations in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and more, according to its store listings. A March 2025 ElevenLabs customer story names ten Indian languages including Marathi, Kannada, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia and Assamese; that is a vendor source, so treat the longer list as partially verified. The app is free to start, with Premium sold as in-app purchases. The same company also ran a separate course site for school children under the Supernova name; that is not the app compared here.

Key differences

The 5 differences that matter most

  1. 1

    Conversation-first vs lesson-first

    SpeakX puts you on a voice call with Sia and teaches through the conversation itself. Supernova runs a fixed daily sequence (pattern, translation drills, controlled chat, free speaking, spaced repetition) with conversation as the final step. Both end in speaking; they start from opposite ends.

  2. 2

    How much of each session is you talking

    On SpeakX the whole session is spoken, and Premium removes the cap with unlimited AI talk time. On Supernova a good share of the 20 minutes goes into translating lines and reviewing earlier material, with the controlled chat and extempore round making up the speaking portion.

  3. 3

    Price transparency

    SpeakX publishes its price on speakx.ai: ₹2 for a 3-day trial, then ₹299/month, cancel anytime. Supernova does not publish a price list on getsupernova.ai; the App Store (India) shows Premium in-app purchases from ₹99 to ₹1,999 and the Play listing shows items from ₹100 to ₹21,200, so you learn the actual plan price inside the app.

  4. 4

    Store footprint

    Supernova has the stronger storefront numbers: 4.7 stars from about 289.5K Google Play reviews and 10M+ downloads, plus 4.7 from about 37.4K App Store (India) ratings. SpeakX shows 4.5 stars from about 92.9K Play reviews and 1 crore+ downloads, with a newer iOS listing. Both figures are as displayed on 12 October 2026.

  5. 5

    Indian-language coverage

    SpeakX names 7 languages on its listings: Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam. Supernova officially names Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali and says 'and more'; a vendor case study lists ten. If you need Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia or Assamese, check inside the Supernova app, since its store pages do not confirm them.

Experience

User experience and how each app gives feedback

On SpeakX, feedback happens during and after the conversation. Sia responds in the moment, and Premium adds a 24x7 AI teacher that gives instant feedback on mistakes. After practice, the Progress Report shows four scores (fluency, vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation) and suggests what to work on next. A Confidence Meter and city leaderboards show how you compare with other learners in your city.

On Supernova, feedback is tied to the lesson structure. Nova provides instant grammar corrections, pronunciation help and personalized feedback, according to the App Store listing, and the controlled-chat stage keeps you inside the pattern you just learned, so corrections stay focused. The spaced-repetition schedule is itself a form of feedback: material returns on a set timetable. A progress dashboard is mentioned in a homepage testimonial but is not documented as a feature, so we cannot describe what it shows.

Neither app offers human tutors; both are AI-only. Gamification such as streaks or badges is not described on Supernova's official pages, while SpeakX offers a Confidence Meter and leaderboards.

On outcomes, SpeakX says that in just 2 months, 4 out of 5 learners start speaking English confidently. That is the company's own claim, not independently verified. Supernova's homepage carries user and rating figures that do not match its store listings on the day we checked, so we have relied on the store listings.

FAQ

SpeakX vs Supernova: common questions

Is Supernova AI or SpeakX better for spoken English practice?+

It depends on how you like to learn. SpeakX suits you if you want to spend the whole session talking on voice calls with Sia in realistic roleplays. Supernova suits you if you want a fixed daily lesson with sentence patterns, translation drills and spaced repetition before you speak freely. Both are Indian apps that explain in Indian languages.

Does SpeakX explain English in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali like Supernova?+

Yes. SpeakX teaches in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali, and also in Marathi, Kannada and Malayalam, 7 Indian languages in total. Supernova officially names Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Bengali and says 'and more'; additional languages appear only in a vendor case study.

What is a good alternative to the Supernova English learning app?+

SpeakX is a close alternative if you want an Indian AI app that explains in your own language but prefer conversation over drills. Compare the practice format (voice calls vs structured lessons), the languages each app names officially, and whether the price is published. Our MySivi and Stimuler comparisons cover two other India-focused options.

How much does SpeakX cost compared with Supernova?+

SpeakX Premium is ₹2 for a 3-day trial and then ₹299 per month, as of 12 October 2026 on the SpeakX website. Supernova does not publish a price list; its App Store (India) in-app purchases range from ₹99 to ₹1,999 and Play items from ₹100 to ₹21,200, so the exact plan price appears inside the app. Both apps are free to start.

Which app has more real-life roleplays, SpeakX or Supernova?+

SpeakX states 100+ real-life scenarios on Premium, including interviews, office conversations, parent-teacher meetings and phone calls. Supernova lists role-plays covering introductions, interviews and everyday situations but does not publish a scenario count. We cannot say Supernova has fewer; only that SpeakX publishes a number and Supernova does not.

Is Supernova AI free?+

Supernova is free to download and start, according to its homepage and store listings, with Premium sold as in-app purchases priced inside the app. SpeakX is also free to start, with Premium at ₹299 per month after a ₹2 3-day trial.

Which Supernova app is this comparison about?+

This page compares SpeakX with Supernova AI: Spoken English by L8 Cones Interactive Private Limited, Bengaluru, on Google Play and the App Store, website getsupernova.ai. It is not about supernova.io (a design-system tool) or other products sharing the name.

Does Supernova track progress like SpeakX's Progress Report?+

SpeakX shows a Progress Report with scores for fluency, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation, plus a Confidence Meter. Supernova's progress tracking is mentioned in a homepage testimonial but not described as a feature on official pages, so we mark it partial rather than missing.
