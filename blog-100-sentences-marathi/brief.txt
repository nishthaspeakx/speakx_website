### H2: How can I learn five English sentences every day? (~250 words)
The four-step drill from the Hindi post: pick five from one situation; say each aloud three times; change one word to make a variation; use at least one in a real conversation the same day. Five a day = the full list in 20 days. Mention the 10-day tracker in the PDF (50 sentences) and how to continue through days 11–20.
### H2: How can I stop translating every sentence from Marathi in my head? (~150 words)
Teach patterns, not one-off lines. Example pattern: "I need…" → I need some water / help / time / rest, once the Marathi meaning has been understood the first time.
### H2: How can I practise daily use English sentences at home? (~200 words)
Read aloud, record on a phone, run imaginary conversations (shop, auto, phone call). Link the live alone-at-home post. Include the 15-minute routine: 5 minutes read, 5 minutes vary, 5 minutes converse.
