5. Outline with section guidance
Intro (~120 words)
Primary keyword in the first sentence. Acknowledge the reader: knows some English from school, can read, freezes when speaking. Promise: 10 steps, a 30-day plan, 15–20 minutes a day.
Answer box: "What is the fastest way to learn English?" (write as below)
The fastest way to learn English is to speak it every day from the first day, not only study grammar. Spend 15–20 minutes daily speaking out loud, learn phrases you will actually use, listen to real English, and get feedback on your mistakes. Most adults who already know basic English notice clearer, more confident speaking within a few weeks of daily practice.


(57 words. The final sentence is a general claim; keep it hedged as written, and do not add a percentage or study unless the writer finds a citable source.)
H2: How fast can you really learn English? (~200 words)
* Set realistic expectations by starting level (beginner / school English / intermediate).
* If citing hour estimates, use only a named source with a link (for example, Cambridge English's published guided-learning-hours guidance for CEFR levels). The writer must quote the exact figures from the source page. No numbers in this brief are verified for this point.
* Include a small table: Level → what "fast" realistically means → focus.
H2: 10 steps to learn English fast (HowTo block, ~900 words, ~80–100 per step)
Each step = H3 + 2–3 sentences + one "Do this today" line.


1. Set one clear goal (interview, office meetings, daily conversation)
2. Speak out loud from day 1 (shadowing, reading aloud)
3. Learn phrases, not single words (link: 100 daily-use sentences post)
4. Think in English for small things (self-talk while cooking or commuting)
5. Listen daily to real English (podcasts, news, series with English subtitles)
6. Learn the grammar you need for speaking (tenses: link to tenses post)
7. Fix pronunciation of the 20 words you use most (link: pronunciation post)
8. Practise conversations with AI (one short paragraph, then link to the ai-se Hinglish page and the AI teacher post)
9. Record yourself weekly and compare
10. Track progress and keep a streak
H2: Your 30-day plan to learn English quickly (~300 words + table)
Table: Week 1–4 × daily task (15–20 min) × goal at end of week. Uses "how to learn english quickly" in the H2.
H2: How to learn English speaking at home (~200 words)
A short version of the practise-alone method, then link to /blog/practice-spoken-english-alone-at-home.
H2: Common mistakes that slow you down (~200 words)
Bulleted list: translating word by word from Hindi, only grammar books, fear of mistakes, no speaking practice, inconsistency.
H2: Practise speaking with SpeakX (~120 words, CTA)
"Reading this won't make you fluent. Speaking will." Practise the day's task out loud in SpeakX and get instant feedback on fluency and pronunciation. Button: Start speaking practice. Do not name the AI tutor (the name is unconfirmed). Use "SpeakX's AI tutor".
H2: FAQ (~350 words), see section 7
Conclusion (~80 words)
One-line recap and the CTA again.
